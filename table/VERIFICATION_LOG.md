## The three dimension failures, resolved 2026-08-23

CA_390_5_7_3, CA_478_5_8_3, CA_1544_5_28_3 (Dwyer, strength 5,
three levels) failed dimension checks. Cause, explained by U.
Groemping (2026-08-24): the Dwyer repository holds two arrays for
some parameter sets, both are listed in DWYERcat, and the dwyerCA
code always fetches the smaller one, so this run's walk over every
catalogue row called dwyerCA for the three larger twins and received
the smaller files, tripping the dimension check. Nothing is wrong
with the repository or the files. Fresh downloads verified all three
arrays at their actual sizes, and 351 and 405 equal the Colbourn
best-known exactly. Resolution rows are appended in the table. The
failed-attempt rows are retained unedited, history is never
rewritten; their notes record the update-in-place guess held at
stamp time, and an interim "cause undetermined" wording, both
superseded by this section.

## Collection cross-check, 2026-08-23

The 224 unique equal-best arrays in arrays/ were re-retrieved
independently on a second machine (x86_64 Linux, R 4.3.3),
re-canonicalized, and re-hashed. All 224 SHA-256 values match the

## Provenance of the verifier itself

caverify 0.2.0 is validated against a pure-R brute-force oracle
sharing no logic with the C kernel, and cross-validated against
CAs::coverage() with exact agreement required on verdicts, gapped
column sets, and missing-tuple counts: 3,541 randomized cases at the
run of record's kernel, including mixed-level arrays, NA-bearing
arrays, and arrays generated by CAs's own constructions, zero
mismatches (xval_mixed.R in this repository). The NA semantics were
corrected 2026-08-23 following a counterexample by Ulrike Groemping;
her example is a permanent regression test in the package.

## Pre-publication deep pass, 2026-08-23

Before publication the assembled artifact was re-audited end to end.
Structural: 14,174 rows parse clean, baseline byte-preserved, every
VERIFIED row stamped and hashed, manifest and files in exact
one-to-one correspondence. Two filename collisions (distinct arrays
sharing parameters) were found and fixed with hash-suffixed names.
Independent re-verification: all 258 shipped array files were re-read
from disk and 207 re-verified exhaustively with ca_verify on a second
machine, zero failures; the remaining 51 (the largest strength-5 and
strength-6 arrays) are covered by SHA-256 identity with the
Mac-minted stamps. Both query helpers agree on 60 randomized queries.

The audit also surfaced 34 VERIFIED arrays whose run counts are
SMALLER than the snapshot's recorded best at their parameters, mostly
Torres-Jimenez arrays shipped in CAs 0.24, plus the two corrected
Dwyer entries. These are improvements present in published sources,
not discoveries of this project; they are included in the collection
under status exceeds_snapshot, with the beaten snapshot value named
in each row's notes.
