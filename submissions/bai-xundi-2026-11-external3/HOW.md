Construction: two constructions of Bai XunDi, "Two-block covering arrays from elliptic curves and curves of genus two" (preprint, 8 November 2026, doi:10.5281/zenodo.24055371). (1) Genus-2 two-block arrays (Theorem 5.2): a curve X: y^2 = f(x) of genus 2 over F_q (q = 23 and q = 13 here) with two rational points at infinity, the space V = L(a Inf+ + b Inf-) of dimension t + 2, and two places Q1, Q2 of degree two; C_i is the subspace of V vanishing at Q_i (dimension t); the rows are all functions of C1 u C2 (2q^t - q^(t-2) of them) evaluated at the rational points of X (42 and 26 points). (2) Elliptic two-block arrays (Theorem 3.1 and Corollary 3.3): an elliptic curve over F_16 with 24 affine points, the space L((t+1)O), and two subspaces of dimension t cut out by a point P0 over F_256; the rows are the functions of their union (2q^t - q^(t-1) of them) evaluated at the 24 affine points and O. Arrays over fewer symbols come from the fusion lemma of Colbourn, Keri, Rivas Soriano and Schlage-Puchta (2010, Lemma 3.1), which removes two rows per symbol; the number of steps is in the construction column.
