Program: https://github.com/tns-research/covering-array-records release v1.9 (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24141927). (1) generators/v1.9/CA_<N>_<t>_<k>_<v>.c, one per array, "gcc -O3 -std=gnu11 -o gen CA_<N>_<t>_<k>_<v>.c -lm; BEST_CANDIDATE=start.txt ./gen > out.txt", where start.txt is the starting array named above. (2) generators/postop.c, "gcc -O3 -march=native -o postop postop.c -lm; ./postop CA_<N>_<t>_<k>_<v>.txt t v <seconds> out.txt" with passes of 300, 1800 and 7200 seconds, each repeated on its own output. The programs of (1) were written by Claude Opus (Anthropic); no LLM runs in them. The searches are randomised and time-limited, so a rerun gives a valid array of similar size.
