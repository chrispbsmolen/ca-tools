Construction: per row in the `construction` column of external.csv. Four constructions, all from doi:10.5281/zenodo.23787051 (paper A) and doi:10.5281/zenodo.23787053 (paper B): (1) Sherwood covering perfect hash families with two rows from Singer cycles of PG(3, q), CPHF(2; 2q+2, q, 4), expanded to covering arrays (Sherwood, Martirosyan and Colbourn 2006), with one extra free column for some arrays (paper B); (2) two-row cyclic and structured CPHFs found by search, expanded the same way (paper B); (3) the poly2 construction with a pencil cover of AG(2, q) (paper A); (4) the elliptic two-block array (paper B, Section 9.2). Arrays over fewer symbols come from the fusion lemma of Colbourn, Keri, Rivas Soriano and Schlage-Puchta (2010, Lemma 3.1), which removes two rows per symbol. Some arrays then had redundant rows deleted (a row is deleted only if the array still covers every t-tuple without it).
Built from: nothing outside the deposit (algebraic constructions and our own CPHF certificates). The certificates (CPHFs, colourings, curves) are in certificates.zip of doi:10.5281/zenodo.23787055, and the arrays are in arrays_t4.zip, arrays_t5.zip and arrays_t6.zip of the same record.
Program: code.zip of doi:10.5281/zenodo.23787055. code/build_cphf_ca.py expands a CPHF certificate into its covering array (CERTIFICATES.md of the deposit gives the commands); code/cacheck.py checks an array exhaustively ("py -3.10 code/cacheck.py arrays/CA_<N>_<t>_<k>_<v>.txt t"); README.md of the deposit, section 3, lists the full verification commands.
