Construction: classic post-optimisation (Nayeri, Colbourn and Konjevod 2013), repeated, of the array named in the construction of each line of external.csv. For CA(N; 4, 48, v), v = 20, 21, 22, the post-optimised array had a redundant first run, which was then deleted. The three files are 62 to 65 MiB, above the 50 MiB limit per file, so they stay in the Zenodo deposit and are named in external.csv.
Built from: the table's CA(559653; 4, 48, 20) and CA(559655; 4, 48, 21) of Bai XunDi, and for v = 22 the table's derived CA(559657; 4, 48, 22), the symbol fusion of the table's CA(559659; 4, 48, 23), a two-row Sherwood CPHF from the Singer cycle of PG(3, 23) in Bai XunDi's preprint (doi:10.5281/zenodo.23787053). Each line repeats the builds_on of its starting array.
Program: https://github.com/tns-research/covering-array-records release v1.10 (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24205260), generators/postop.c, "gcc -O3 -march=native -o postop postop.c -lm; ./postop CA_<N>_<t>_<k>_<v>.txt t v <seconds> out.txt" with passes of 300, 1800 and 7200 seconds, each repeated on its own output. No LLM runs in it. The search is randomised and time-limited, so a rerun gives a valid array of similar size.
